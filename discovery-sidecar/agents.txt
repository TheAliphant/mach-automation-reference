MACH Discovery Lab
MODE=LAB_ONLY
PRODUCTION_CONNECTED=false

Purpose:
Study how autonomous agents search for, compare, select, test and describe capabilities.

Discovery:
GET /v1/discover?task=<intent>

Quote:
GET /v1/quote?capability_id=<id>

Sandbox:
POST /v1/probe
Only lab.mock.* capabilities are accepted.

Execution:
POST /v1/execute
Always disabled. Returns production_execution_disabled.

Feedback:
POST /v1/report-outcome
Use outcome values such as success, partial, wrong_format, too_slow, too_expensive, not_found.
